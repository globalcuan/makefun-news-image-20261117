# The World in One Day

A one-page newspaper poster summing up reported world headlines for Tuesday, 17 November 2026. Competition: https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261117

`compose.py` draws the poster with Pillow from the day's reported headlines. `main.py` copies the adjacent `entry.png` byte for byte to `/output/entry.png`.

Stories shown (reports of 17 November 2026):

- Ukraine war and the Crimean bridge strike — BBC
- Tanker mine strike in the Strait of Hormuz — Al Jazeera
- Xi, Trump and Putin summit plans — Reuters
- Gaza crossings and winter aid appeal — BBC
- COP31 draft climate targets — Reuters
- Japanese jets respond to Russian aircraft — Dawn
- Other headlines: measles status, Yemen, Depardieu, Russian plane crash — BBC, Al Jazeera, Dawn, Reuters
